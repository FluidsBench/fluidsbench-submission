# DrivAerML opening decision — 28 October 2026

Neil Ashton, acting as the DrivAerML dataset owner, directed FluidsBench to
open DrivAerML submissions without the previously proposed three-model
ranking-sensitivity study. This supersedes that study as an activation gate;
it does not claim that the study or bootstrap was run. The fixed-field error
caps, bounded profile scores, and zero-weight relative diagnostics remain as
already defined in the reviewed candidate contract.

Daniel Leibovici's independently prepared CANTO Full submission covers all 50
official Full test cases with genuine model inference and a validated schema-v3
package. It supplies the requested complete-split submission trial. Its
pre-release registration is evidence of the workflow, not an official result
to carry into the new, empty repository.

The official support is published, participant packaging and contributor validation are enabled, and the leaderboard starts empty. The scientific approval and support bindings are recorded in `submission-spec.json`.
